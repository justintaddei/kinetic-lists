# One Household stream per tab, authorized by the tables' own RLS, with the write's ACK as local truth and v1 online-only

> **Records the live layer [ADR-0016](0016-buy-takes-the-whole-basket-and-claims-rows-on-the-servers-clock.md) left open** (its amendment to [#8](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/8): a Buy's report rides the RPC reply, a co-Member's Buy moves rows via `postgres_changes`), **walks through [ADR-0023](0023-the-v1-schema-is-ten-household-owned-tables-under-pair-keys-with-three-granted-definer-rpcs-as-its-whole-privileged-surface.md)'s grant posture without adding to it**, and **amends the chrome language of [ADR-0024](0024-the-shell-is-a-tab-bar-spine-of-six-tabbed-routes-plus-sheets-with-the-44px-floor-in-the-copied-components.md)**. It absorbs the question "Define offline edits and synchronization for shared Household data" ([#28](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/28), merged). The whole answer is [#8's resolution](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/8#issuecomment-6564280833); this names the load-bearing cuts.

Kinetic Lists v1 has **one Realtime channel per browser tab, scoped to the viewer's Household** — topic `household:{householdId}`, filtered `household_id=eq.{householdId}` (`id=eq.` on `households`). **A per-List channel is dead**: `buy_basket` writes rows in every List at once, and the Kinetic View, the Basket, the rosters and the per-List screens are all cross-List. One Household per Member (`unique (user_id)`, ADR-0014) and no switcher make the topic **a constant per session**. The topic is **not a secret and not an access rule**.

**The stream is a publication, and authorization is the source tables' RLS — nothing new.** `postgres_changes` sends "only to clients who are allowed to read them based on your RLS policies", on public and private channels alike, so v1 writes **no** `realtime.messages` policy, uses **no** `private: true` channel, needs **no** hosted toggle, **no** definer helper, and **no** new grant: the socket is a reader, and the tables' `select` policies are the door. Eight tables join the publication — `list_items`, `items`, `lists`, `stores`, `store_locations`, `household_members`, `households`, `item_store_tags` — because they are what a live screen reads. Four stay out: `arrivals` (no v1 screen reads it), `purchases` and `purchase_lines` (closed to the browser, no history screen), `household_invites` (its `token` is the Membership). **Every published table takes `REPLICA IDENTITY FULL`** (seven new lines; `item_store_tags` already has it): under `DEFAULT` a `DELETE` payload is the PK alone, so `household_id=eq.` cannot filter a delete, and **RLS is not applied to `DELETE` events at all** — the filter plus `FULL` is what we control, and it is the mechanism behind ADR-0022's "co-Members watch rows vanish live". Bloat is accepted for payload visibility, recorded as a standing verdict in `docs/agents/advisors.md`.

**The write's ACK is local truth; the stream is authoritative.** A tap moves its own row on the write's `2xx` (Check, Need, and a Buy's claimed rows from `buy_basket`'s reply), and **every stream event lands as it arrives, this device's own echo included** — the event payload *is* the row's new truth, so applying it needs no event-identity tracking, no dedup and no precedence rule; two Members writing one row is the ordinary case, and ADR-0016's claim race is answered inside the RPC. A write's failure **is** the report, and the optimistic row flips back. Delivery is best-effort with **no durable replay and no catch-up API**, so **refetch on (re)subscribe and on focus/visibility** is v1's entire synchronization story — silent, with **no staleness chrome, no connection badge, no offline affordance**.

**v1 is online-only, and offline has no name.** Every affordance — Check, Buy, add, edit, Tag, Pick — is an **online act**: one call, and its failure is its own report. An offline **Check fails fast**, and **Buy is an online act** and fails the same way: `purchased_at` is `default now()` on the server, so a queued replay names the reconnect's minute, not the shelf's, and its report rides a reply that has not happened. **No queue, no merge rules, no freshness/replay rules, no sync UI, no connection language.**

**First paint stays client-only, and the subscription lives in one deep module.** `app/plugins/live.ts` (client-only) plus one composable own the socket; **the shell layout mounts it and unmounts it**, and screens read local row state — they never touch a channel. SSR paints the shell, rows are fetched at mount, and **no socket exists in SSR**; `server/api` gains **no** Realtime seam (supabase-js carries the session's token; `setAuth` is for custom JWTs).

**Two pieces of ADR-0024's copy die here.** The Kinetic View's **last-Bought line is dropped** (`Bought 5 at Big Y (Home Main) · Justin · 2h`): `purchases` has no read door for `authenticated`, `list_items.stocked_at` gives the hour and nothing else, and the map's own Out-of-scope already ships a "last Bought" line post-v1 — so the column ends at the gate copy and count. The **co-Member Buy claim toast is dropped** with it: co-Members keep **no announcement surface** (ADR-0022's rule, held from #20 forward); the top-center Toaster mount keeps a client — **a write's own report**, of which ADR-0016's `23514` report (naming the co-Member and their Store, drawn by the losing Member's own reply) is the one v1 has. Its copy strings are [#32](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/32)'s. The `(Home Main)` parenthetical is stale independently: **a Location has no name** (ADR-0017, ADR-0021).

**Consequences.** The schema bill is 7 `replica identity full` lines plus publication membership ([#29](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/29), whose first step is verifying whether pg-delta models publications), the live layer is [#30](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/30), the copy edits are [#31](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/31), and [#27](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/27) inherits seven Realtime assertions — the co-Member row moving live with zero toasts, a cascade erasure landing live, a blocked socket failing a write and a re-join refetching it, zero events for a signed-out visitor and for a foreign topic, one WebSocket per tab. The hosted checklist on [#10](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/10) gains two lines: **Realtime stays public** (keep "Allow public access" ON) and a **short JWT expiry**, because a connection caches its policy claims until its token expires (local `jwt_expiry = 3600`). Delivery latency is assumed, not measured: **Free-plan viability is assumed at 2 planned users** (Free = 200 peak connections, 2M messages/month, 256 KB max message, one connection per tab); a larger Household is Justin's revisit. What v1 promises and never says is its own connection state — a later offline queue, a sync UI, an "Offline" line, a `list:{id}` channel, a private channel, a `realtime.messages` policy, a `purchases` subscription, a co-Member Buy toast, or a last-Bought line breaks this decision rather than extending it.
