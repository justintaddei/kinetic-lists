# Hygiene verbs live on the entity's own surface, and a cascade confirmation names only what the Household can see

> **Amends [ADR-0017](0017-purchase-lines-reference-rows-and-every-delete-cascades.md):** the confirmations it deferred to this ticket are now designed — three of them, naming live effects in counts.
> **Amends [ADR-0018](0018-store-and-item-names-are-household-unique-by-index.md):** for a **rename onto a taken name**, an **Item** collision is **refused**, not offered a join and not adopted. The index's ruling is unchanged; the app's answer to `23505` differs by entity.

[ADR-0017](0017-purchase-lines-reference-rows-and-every-delete-cascades.md) shipped five verbs — **Delete** a Store, a Location, an Item; **Rename** a Store, an Item — with a full cascade whose reach RLS never checks, and left the surfaces and the confirmations to [ticket #20](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/20). The verbs land on **the entity's own surface**: a **Store screen** (rename, Tags, its Locations, delete), the **Item sheet** (rename, Category, Quantity, Tags, delete), and the Household tab's **Store roster** and **Item roster** — the **Catalogue** at last with a home — whose rows are the doors to those surfaces. A drawer that lists what you are tidying, not what you are Picking: [ADR-0021](0021-the-active-store-picker-lists-stores-only-and-an-arrival-names-the-location.md) ruled the picker out as a hygiene surface, and a **Household hygiene screen** (one screen owning every verb) was rejected because the entity surfaces already exist and a roster without per-entity screens is the same screen by another name.

**The Store screen is one screen describing one Store**, reached from the Kinetic View's header and from the Store roster. Its name row opens the **mint sheet** (ADR-0021's one component) on its edit door — the only door carrying a **name field**, Position-first as always — and a name taken opens #13's name-join sheet, index `23505` the backstop. Its Locations are edited **inline, on the screen**: one row each, `42.2562, -71.7799 · 150 m`, Geofence radius and coordinates editable there, since a **Location has no name** and deserves no sheet of its own. `+ Add a Location from where I am` opens the mint sheet with the Store's name fixed — ADR-0021's five doors, the fifth being the screen of the Store you are on, which makes a **Bare Store** curable from its own screen and not only from Buy's ask.

**A cascade confirmation names only what the Household can see, in counts.** Three exist in all of v1, each an owned **Alert Dialog** with a destructive **Delete**; everything else in v1 — rename, Geofence, coordinates, Tags, List rename, Household rename, Display Name — saves without a confirmation:

- Store: *"Deletes Walmart and its 2 Locations, unpins it from 12 Items, and removes 9 Needed rows — for every Member."*
- Item: *"Removes Milk from 3 Lists and 2 Stores, for every Member."*
- Location: *"Delete this Location from Walmart? 1 of 3 Locations."*

Nothing else the cascade erases is mentioned. **Purchases, Purchase Lines and Arrivals go in silence** — ADR-0016 ships no history screen and no report reads a Purchase, so a "92 Buys" count warns about a thing the Member cannot see, cannot miss, and cannot check. The **Standing Location** and the **Active Store** are named in no confirmation either ([ADR-0021](0021-the-active-store-picker-lists-stores-only-and-an-arrival-names-the-location.md)'s amendment to this ticket): a cascade that erases only browser state erases only what the Member's next screen re-derives, and the fall to **no Active Store** is the fresh-Household state, not a crisis. **No gate** on any of the three: no typed name, no co-Member warning, no preview list — the sheet is a Member's own warning, not a negotiation, and [ADR-0017](0017-purchase-lines-reference-rows-and-every-delete-cascades.md) already refused gating a cascade in the database.

**Item discovery is a verb's requirement, not a nicety.** Refusing an Item rename means the loser row is cleaned up by deletion, and the Kinetic View (Needed at one Store) and Lists (what's on them) show a typo'd Item to nobody. So the **Item roster** exists: the Household tab's list of the Catalogue — `Milk · 3 Lists · Walmart, Big Y` — beside the Store roster's `Walmart · 2 Locations`, filter field at six rows (ADR-0021's threshold, reused), the row a single tap target opening its sheet, no swipe and no row buttons.

**An Item rename onto a taken name is refused** — the sheet answers *"Milk already exists"* and stops. No adoption, no merge, no link to the existing Item: [ticket #7](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/7)'s "adopt, do not offer a duplicate" is superseded for the rename path, because adoption is an **Item merge** — the loser's live List Items folded into the survivor's Lists (a List ending with two Milk rows), its Tags unioned, its Category and Quantity discarded, its history Lines re-pointed — and [ADR-0018](0018-store-and-item-names-are-household-unique-by-index.md)'s answer for a duplicate is always **delete the loser**. Capture's collision path is untouched (name exists → add Tags to the existing Item); refusal only guards the rename, where the Member is holding a name and a sheet.

**Aftermath is quiet.** The actor gets a toast naming their erasure (*"Deleted Walmart"*); co-Members get none — their Kinetic View, picker rosters and Lists simply lose the rows, live, on #8's Realtime seam, in the vocabulary of [ADR-0016](0016-buy-takes-the-whole-basket-and-claims-rows-on-the-servers-clock.md)'s Buy race report that writes nothing. There is no Undo in v1: a cascade that erases history nothing reads is not worth a replay mechanism that has to re-create it.

Consequences by ticket: **#3** serves five single-row writes under RLS (2 deletes, 2 renames, Location edits — no RPC, ADR-0017) and the rosters' read policies, plus the cascade index list unchanged; **#11** prototypes the Store screen, the Item sheet, both rosters, the three confirmations and the post-delete empty state; **#21** styles the roster row, the destructive verb, and the three sheets; **#13**'s modal confirm is now the owned **Alert Dialog**, and its join sheet survives as the Store rename's collision offer; **#6**'s Standing Location outliving its Location is the same null fall as a deleted Active Store; **#4**'s seam is role-and-name over a roster (`getByRole('button', { name: 'Milk' })`) plus the Alert Dialog's confirmed-delete click, never Escape.

Rejected, and why: **a Household hygiene screen** (rosters plus a screen owning every verb — the entity surfaces already exist, and a list of Stores with Delete buttons in a picker-shaped screen is what #19 already refused); **adopt-and-merge on an Item rename** (an Item merge verb, a merge sheet, and a preview of rows the Member is not looking at, to fix a typo the roster fixes with one Delete); **a Location sheet** (nameless coordinate rows are 2 fields and a delete); **a Location delete with no confirm** (it is the Household's place; one tap of confirmation is the floor); **status counts in the copy** (the sheet the Member is reading already shows the Tags and the Lists; naming "1 In Basket" in an erasure sentence turns a shared row into an accusation); **the co-Member gate** (a Member's own cleanup negotiating with people who are not in the room); **an Undo toast** (it needs a payload, a replay, and history that isn't disposable); **a List delete verb** (Lists are the Household's names for standing sets, ADR-0001; its cascade takes standing rows only, and v1 shipped no List rename collision to justify its delete — fog, not a verb); **a delete announcement naming the Member who erased a shared row** (a notification surface v1 has no room for, answering a cascade that names no one).
