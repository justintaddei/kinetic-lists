# The shell is a tab-bar spine of six tabbed routes plus sheets, with the 44px floor in the copied components

> **Amends [ADR-0020](0020-the-ui-is-shadcn-vue-components-copied-into-the-app-on-tailwind-v4-phone-first.md):** `Button size="lg"` is `h-10` (**40px**, measured in the copy), not `h-11` — the claim this decision inherits was wrong. The 44px floor is the copy's **lifted defaults** (`h-9` → `h-11` on `Button`, `Input`, `InputGroup`, `SelectTrigger`), so a bare control is 44px and no call site writes a `size`.
> **Records the shell over the surfaces [ADR-0013](0013-a-store-has-locations-and-its-coordinates-come-from-a-position.md), [ADR-0015](0015-store-tags-are-written-at-capture-and-an-arrival-selects-the-active-store.md), [ADR-0016](0016-buy-takes-the-whole-basket-and-claims-rows-on-the-servers-clock.md), [ADR-0021](0021-the-active-store-picker-lists-stores-only-and-an-arrival-names-the-location.md) and [ADR-0022](0022-hygiene-verbs-live-on-the-entity-s-own-surface-and-a-cascade-confirmation-names-only-what-the-household-can-see.md) fixed**, and the patterns [ticket #13](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/13) settled. The whole answer is [#21's resolution](https://github.com/justintaddei/kinetic-lists/issues/21); this names the load-bearing cuts.

Kinetic Lists v1 has **one shell**: a named Nuxt layout (`app/layouts/app.vue`) carrying a **tab bar**, a **Capture FAB**, the Kinetic View's **header** slot, and two **Toaster** mounts. Six **tabbed routes** — Kinetic View, Lists, Basket, Household, and the per-List screen reachable from Lists — plus one **detail route** (the **Store screen**) and one signed-out route (`/join/[token]`; landing, `/login`, `/confirm` wear no chrome) are **the whole v1 navigation**. Everything else is a **sheet**: **Capture, Item, Store picker, Mint, List editor, Household editor, Member editor, Invite, name-join** — nine, all Drawers, all opening **below the tab bar**, all capped at **`max-h-[86vh]`**. **Three Alert Dialogs** exist (the **Cascade confirmations**) and the centred Dialog ships in the **owned set** and on **no v1 phone surface**. **The tab bar, not a header, says where you are**, and **nothing in v1 opens a modal layer** — the name-join offer rides in a sheet.

**The 44px floor lives in the copied components, not at call sites.** Five owned files are edited once — `Button`, `Input`, `InputGroup`, `SelectTrigger` default `h-9` → `h-11`, and `DrawerContent`'s swipe-down cap `mt-24 max-h-[80vh]` → `max-h-[86vh]` — and the `theme-color` metas follow the measured tokens (`#ffffff` / `#111111`). `xs`/`sm`/`lg` stay untouched so 32–40px small bits (the Dialog close) remain available. **Data rows** are 48px (`min-h-12`) with `UiSeparator` hairlines: a name in `text-base`, one **quiet line** in `text-sm text-muted-foreground` joined by `·` (`Walmart · 2 Locations`), and **no chips, badges or chevrons on data rows**.

**A sheet edits live and its footer carries only the destructive verb.** Category/Quantity are `UiInput`s sugaring off a native `list` **datalist** (the owned set has no combobox and v1 adds no registry component); Store Tag capture is **`aria-pressed` pills** (outline `h-11`, `LucideCheck` when ticked, the standing-at Store pre-ticked per #13); sections are separated by hairlines and content order, **with no micro-label headings inside a sheet**. The Item/Store sheets have **no Save/Cancel pair** (writes land on touch), Close sits in the header beside the entity's title, the destructive verb is an `outline` button in `text-destructive`, and **the only red in v1 is the action verb of a Cascade confirmation**. **`UiEmpty` is v1's one emptiness furniture** — its title names the count — used for all six emptinesses, the **no-Store first run** being the Kinetic View's empty state carrying the **Mint** control. The **Check** affordance is a row-leading 44px ghost icon control everywhere rows live (**Kinetic View**, **per-List**, **Basket**), and a row tap opens the **Item sheet**.

**The Kinetic View carries the app's only header, with exactly two controls**: the ghost Store-name control (opens the **Store picker**; `aria-pressed` ticks when an **Arrival** named a **Standing Location**; reads `No Store picked` on the first run) and, when the Household owns one, a 44px ghost icon control opening the **Store screen**. **No copy line, no Location line, no tab title.** Its column reads: header → **filter field** at six rows → flat rows → the **gate copy** and count as its **last quiet line** → one **last-Bought line** (`Bought 5 at Big Y (Home Main) · Justin · 2h`, #16's promise kept). The **FAB** is a `size-11` circle opening the **Capture sheet**, on every tab. Counts live in chrome: the Basket tab's label carries `· 3`. Co-Member claims toast from a **top-center** Toaster offset under the header; the Buy report and the Undo toast from a **bottom-center** one.

**Desktop is the same column in a container, not a layout** (ADR-0020): every surface, the chrome and the sheets ride a `max-w-md` (448px) column, `mx-auto`, **full-bleed** (no page gutter; rows inset their own `px-4`), capped once at `max-h-[86vh]` so one cap applies and a sheet never scrolls inside a scroll. Safe-area padding lives **only on chrome** — header `pt-safe-offset-2`, tab bar `pt-safe-offset-4`, sheet footer `pb-safe-offset-4`, the Toaster's offset — so no screen content pad floats off the edge, and the **FAB** floats 16px above the tab bar's hairline, clearing the column's last quiet line. **Landing, `/login` and `/confirm` restyle to utility classes and tokens with the scoped CSS, hex colors, and their hand-rolled `.button` deleted, `<UiButton>` carrying the OAuth verb at 44px, and no chrome** — ADR-0020's deferred restyle, now planned.

The seams the Playwright suite already drives (role + accessible name, dismiss by clicking) extend without new kinds: the tab bar is a labelled `nav` with `aria-current="page"`, the filter inputs are labelled `Filter Stores` / `Filter Items`, Tag pills and rows are accessible by their labels, the Close by its name, the two toast regions by copy. **No new `data-testid` is added.**

**Consequences.** The tab bar is a v1 promise a later nav change must break; the sheet set is fixed, so a new verb needs an existing sheet; the `max-h-[86vh]` cap and the lifted defaults are **app-owned edits upstream will churn**, and every new registry control has to arrive at 44px defaults and be labelled, because v1 writes no size and no icon-only control. **Ticket #11 (the Kinetic View prototype) is unblocked and inherits this chrome as its brief**; #6, #7, #8, #10 and #27 inherit it in their seams.
