# Buy takes the whole Basket, claims rows on the server's clock, and writes through one RPC

[ADR-0010](0010-a-list-item-has-one-status.md) made **Buy** one Member's act on the whole Basket at the Picked Store, and [ADR-0015](0015-store-tags-are-written-at-capture-and-an-arrival-selects-the-active-store.md) made the **Active Store** an **Arrival**'s choice, which left the shop-floor questions: what a Buy does to the rows a co-Member touched at another Store, which **Location** a **Purchase** names when nobody arrived anywhere, what a tap promises when it lands twice, and where a multi-row write with an invariant lives. We decided: **Buy takes every List Item the Household has In Basket at the Active Store** — v1 has no partial Buy and no per-row selection, and the exit is the Basket itself (a Member who no longer wants an Item takes it out of the Basket, an edge of the Status triangle v1 deliberately names no act for); a Buy claims **only the rows still In Basket at write time**, guarded by `status = 'in_basket' AND stocked_at > bought_at` with **every timestamp on the server's clock** (the transaction's start), so the first Buy to reach a row records it and the Member whose Buy found it already Bought records nothing for it and hears about it in a toast naming the co-Member and their Store; the write is one RPC, `private.buy_basket`, carrying a **session token** (one per Basket visit, claimed by the write, unique per Household) so a replayed token returns the recorded Purchase and writes nothing; and a Buy with nothing to claim is refused, so a Purchase always has at least one **Purchase Line**.

We chose the claim guard over last-write-wins because a **Purchase** is a record of what one Member bought at one Store ([ADR-0004](0004-purchases-are-append-only-snapshots.md)), and a whole-Basket Buy that re-stocks a co-Member's row on a phone with clock skew is the same act recorded twice; and we chose the RPC because a client holding three `supabase-js` calls cannot own a transaction boundary, a guard and a token claim at once, while a `server/api` route in front of the RPC would be a pass-through with one adapter (a hypothetical seam). We accepted the loss the guard causes: *Alex also bought milk at Big Y* is unrecordable in v1's single **Status**, because milk is one shared row and the first writer owns its snapshot.

Consequences:

- **A Location is never minted silently** — the app asks. A **bare Store**'s Buy offers the mint, and declining is legal: a Purchase's Location is nullable, and a Purchase Line snapshots the Store name plus the Location name *where one was known*. That amends [ADR-0013](0013-a-store-has-locations-and-its-coordinates-come-from-a-position.md)'s "a Purchase names the Store *and* the Location".
- **The no-Active-Store hole in ADR-0015.** "A Member who opens the app at a Store lands straight on it" and the home screen's "+ Store from where I am" offer left a hole: a Household that has captured **no Store** has nothing to Pick, so it has no Active Store. We chose to keep the Active Store optional and to give the hole a gate — **Buy is never gated by a Pick**. In that state Buy opens "name this shop", minting its one Store (name + one-shot Position, in a sheet the Member's tap confirms, and a bare Store when the Position is denied), so a Household's first Buy names its first Store.
- **Buy mints in the hole, nowhere else.** Every other Buy — an Arrival's Store, a hand-Picked Store, a bare Store she declines to pin — writes no Store and no Location, leaving Store minting in two places (the header's sheet and Buy's gate) to cover two different situations: shopping at a Store that exists, and shopping where none does.
- **v1 shows no Purchase history.** The Kinetic View shows **Needed** only, so history has no home. A toast naming a co-Member's Store is the only surface a Buy leaves, and it writes nothing (ADR-0015's report rule). v1 records Purchases and Purchase Lines that no Member will ever read in the app.
