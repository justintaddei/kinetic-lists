# Store Tags are written at capture, Buy offers nothing, and an Arrival selects the Active Store

Store Tag authoring had two answers on the map at once: [ADR-0013](0013-a-store-has-locations-and-its-coordinates-come-from-a-position.md) decided that a freshly captured Item carries **no Store Tags** — tags arrive by shopping, by picking, and by the **Tag** offer at Buy — and [ADR-0005](0005-arrival-is-client-side-and-only-suggests.md) decided that an **Arrival** only ever *suggests*, with the **Active Store** set by hand. The round-2 prototype of the capture form and the Tag offer (wayfinder ticket #13, branch `prototype/store-tag-authoring`) put both in front of Justin on a phone frame, and both were rejected where they stand: the capture form wants the Store in it, and a suggestion the user never acts on is not a suggestion.

We chose to write **Store Tags at capture** — the capture form lists the Household's Stores as chips, the Store the Member is standing at arrives pre-ticked, and "+ Add a store from where I am now" sits under them — and to **retire the Tag offer at Buy**: Buy records a **Purchase** and marks List Items **Stocked**, and prompts for nothing. The gate a Tag-less Item creates is stated **where the Item is created** (the capture form names the Items nobody will see at a Store) and counted **in the Basket**; after a Buy the flow may *report* the bought Items with no Tag for that Store, and a report writes nothing. We chose this because the burden ADR-0013 was protecting capture from is exactly the burden the shopper wants to pay once: naming where a thing is bought, at the moment of naming the thing. A Buy-time interstitial sits in a flow whose whole job is to record, and the name-join confirmation it would need already exists on the capture side.

This retires the **Tag** act as an offer at Buy (**Tag** survives as the act of linking an Item to a Store, written at capture, on the Item's screen, and on the Store's screen), and amends [ADR-0013](0013-a-store-has-locations-and-its-coordinates-come-from-a-position.md) ("no Store Tag at all for a freshly captured Item") and [ADR-0002](0002-items-are-per-user-shared-by-list.md)'s tag-by-shopping reasoning. It amends [ADR-0005](0005-arrival-is-client-side-and-only-suggests.md) in one specific way: an **Arrival** now **selects the Active Store** — the Store of the **Location** the Member is Inside becomes the app's default screen — and still **moves no List Item**: nothing is Picked, no Status changes, and the Member overrides the choice from the header, their pick standing until the next Arrival. That default *is* the suggestion ADR-0005 promised, made visible; "never automatic" is dropped, "and only suggests" is kept in the sense that survives it.

Consequences: the app's home screen is the **Kinetic View of the Active Store**, and a Member who opens the app at a Store lands straight on it; when **no Location** matches the Position, the home screen offers to **add a Store from where they are standing**, so the app is never left without an Active Store and without a way out; a **bare Store** (no Locations) can never be selected by an Arrival and stays Pickable by name; Store Tags are edited in three places — the capture form, the Item's screen, the Store's screen — and **not** on Kinetic View or Basket rows, so those views stay shopping views and never become Tag editors. The Buy flow's shape (which Store, which Location, append-only Purchase, Purchase Lines snapshotting both) is unchanged, as is the append-only Purchase of [ADR-0004](0004-purchases-are-append-only-snapshots.md) and the tri-**Status** List Item of [ADR-0010](0010-a-list-item-has-one-status.md). Prototype answers that are UI vocabulary, not data rules, stay on the ticket: the tab bar spine, the FAB, forms in bottom sheets, the modal confirm sheet for name-join.
